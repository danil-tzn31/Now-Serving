# Requesting a birth certificate for someone else

## Summary
Birth records are confidential. Besides the person themselves, only their
spouse, parents, children or grandchildren, legal guardian, or someone they've
authorized can request a copy. Anyone else needs an authorization letter or
SPA from the owner.

## Who this is for
Anyone requesting, or receiving the delivery of, a birth certificate that
isn't their own.

## What you need
**Who's allowed, per Article 7 of the Child and Youth Welfare Code:**
1. The person themselves, or anyone they authorize
2. Their spouse, parent or parents, or direct descendants
3. Their guardian or the institution legally in charge of them, if a minor
4. A court or proper public official, when absolutely necessary in official
   proceedings
5. The nearest of kin, if the person has died

**If you're an authorized representative:**
- In person: an authorization letter or Special Power of Attorney (SPA) from
  the owner, plus valid IDs of both the owner and you
- Online delivery through PSA Helpline: the owner assigns you through the
  Online Letter of Authorization (LOA), and you show a valid ID to the courier

## Steps
1. Check that you're on the list above, or get authorization from the owner.
2. Request it online or in person (see `psa-birth-cert-online` or
   `psa-birth-cert-in-person`), stating your relationship to the person.

## Fees
Same as a normal request: ₱155 in person, ₱330 to ₱365 online with delivery.

## Where to apply
Online or at a PSA CRS outlet.

## How long it takes
Same as a normal request.

## Common problems
- **You're a sibling, cousin or friend:** you're not on the list, so you need
  the owner's authorization letter or SPA.
- **Passing it on:** an authorized representative can't hand the task to
  someone else.
- **Requesting for many people:** a representative can request for at most
  2 unrelated people at a PSA outlet.

## Not covered here
- Penalties: releasing birth records to people not on the list is punishable
  under Article 7, which is why outlets are strict about it.
