# Fixing a mistake on a birth certificate

## Summary
Typos, a first name you want changed, the wrong sex, or the wrong day or month
of birth can be fixed without going to court. File a petition at the civil
registry office where the birth is registered. It costs ₱1,000 for a clerical
error and ₱3,000 for the other changes.

## Who this is for
People whose birth certificate has:
- a clerical or typographical error (RA 9048)
- a first name or nickname they need changed (RA 9048)
- the wrong sex, or the wrong day or month of birth (RA 10172)

## What you need
- At least two public or private documents showing the correct details
- Any other documents the civil registrar asks for

**Who can file:** the person themselves if of legal age, or their spouse,
children, parents, siblings, grandparents, guardian, or someone authorized by
law or by the person (with an SPA).

## Steps
1. Go to the civil registry office where the birth certificate is
   registered. If the birth was reported abroad, go to the Philippine
   consulate where it was reported.
2. File the petition with your supporting documents and pay the fee.
3. Follow the registrar's instructions until the petition is decided.

## Fees
As of 2026-10-26, per the PSA page:
- Clerical error (RA 9048): ₱1,000
- Change of first name, or sex and birth day or month (RA 10172): ₱3,000
- At a Philippine consulate: US$50 (clerical) or US$150 (first name, RA 10172)
- Filing somewhere other than where the birth is registered (migrant
  petition): ₱500 or ₱1,000 extra

## Where to apply
The civil registry office where the birth is registered, or the Philippine
consulate where it was reported.

## How long it takes
Not stated on the PSA page.

## Common problems
- **You need it for a passport:** the DFA asks for a PSA-annotated birth
  certificate showing the correction. See `passport-new-adult`.
- **The mistake is bigger than a typo:** changes to your surname or middle
  name, nationality, parents' names, year of birth, or legitimacy need a court
  case, not a petition at the civil registrar.

- **After approval:** request a new PSA copy, online or in person, to get the
  corrected certificate.

## Not covered here
- How long the petition takes: it varies. Follow up with the civil registrar
  handling your petition.
- Court cases for bigger changes: ask a lawyer or the Public Attorney's Office.
