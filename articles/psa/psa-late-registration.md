# When PSA has no record of your birth

## Summary
If your birth was never registered, file a late registration at the Local
Civil Registrar (LCR) of the place where you were born. Once it's processed,
you can request a PSA copy marked as late registered.

## Who this is for
People whose birth was never registered, or who were told PSA has no record
of them.

## What you need
The documents for late registration are set by the Local Civil Registrar and
aren't listed on the PSA or DFA pages. Ask the LCR of the city or
municipality where you were born.

## Steps
1. Go to the Local Civil Registrar of the place where you were born. If you
   were born abroad, go to the Philippine consular office covering that place.
2. File for late registration and submit what they ask for.
3. Once registered, request your PSA birth certificate. Say it was registered
   late and give the year (see `psa-birth-cert-online`).

## Fees
Not stated on the PSA or DFA pages. Ask your Local Civil Registrar.

## Where to apply
The Local Civil Registrar of your place of birth.

## How long it takes
Not stated on the PSA or DFA pages.

## Common problems
- **You got a "Negative Certification" from PSA:** it means PSA has no record
  of your birth in its archives. Bring it to the Local Civil Registrar where
  your birth should have been registered and ask what to do next.
- **You need it for a passport:** the DFA asks for a late-registered PSA birth
  certificate plus one more primary ID, or two supporting documents such as
  an NBI clearance or school records. See `passport-new-adult`.

## Not covered here
- Fixing a mistake on a birth certificate that already exists: see
  `psa-corrections`.
