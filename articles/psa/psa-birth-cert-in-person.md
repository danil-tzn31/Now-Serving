# Getting a PSA birth certificate in person

## Summary
Book a free appointment at appointment.psa.gov.ph, then go to your chosen
PSA CRS outlet with a valid ID. A copy costs ₱155 and is released on the
date shown on your receipt.

## Who this is for
The person on the certificate, or someone allowed to request it for them, who
wants to get a copy at a PSA outlet.

## What you need
- An appointment slip from https://appointment.psa.gov.ph/ (printed or on
  your phone)
- A valid ID that matches the name you used when booking. Accepted IDs
  include the PhilSys ID or ePhilID, passport, driver's license, PRC ID,
  UMID (SSS or GSIS), Pag-IBIG card, voter's ID, postal ID, senior citizen ID,
  and OFW or OWWA ID.
- If requesting for someone else: an authorization letter or Special Power of
  Attorney (SPA), plus the IDs of both the document owner and you
- The details of the person on the certificate (full name, parents' names,
  date and place of birth)

## Steps
1. Book an appointment at https://appointment.psa.gov.ph/. Pick the date,
   time and CRS outlet.
2. Arrive at least 30 minutes early and show your appointment slip to the
   information marshal.
3. Get an application form and a queue number, and fill out the form.
4. At the window, submit the form, your IDs, any authorization letter or SPA,
   and pay.
5. Check your official receipt for the release date.
6. On that date, go to the releasing area with your receipt and IDs, and
   check the certificate for errors before leaving.

## Fees
As of 2026-10-26: ₱155 per copy.

## Where to apply
At PSA CRS outlets (Census Serbilis Centers). You pick the outlet when
booking. Requests made at the East Avenue outlet are released there.

## How long it takes
The release date is printed on your official receipt.

## Common problems
- **No appointment:** walk-in requests require one. Booking is free.
- **Minors:** people under 18 can't book an appointment.
- **The appointment slip:** it can't be transferred to someone else.
- **Representatives:** an authorized representative can't pass the task on to
  another person, and can only request for up to 2 unrelated people.

## Not covered here
- Ordering online with delivery: see `psa-birth-cert-online`.
- No birth record found: see `psa-late-registration`.
- Mistakes on the certificate: see `psa-corrections`.
