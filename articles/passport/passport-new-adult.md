# Getting a passport for the first time (adults)

## Summary
Book an online appointment with the DFA, then go in person with your PSA
birth certificate, one valid ID and the filled-out application form. Regular
processing costs ₱950 and takes 12 business days.

## Who this is for
Filipinos aged 18 and over applying for their very first passport. If you've
had a passport before, even an expired one, see `passport-renewal-adult`.

## What you need
- A confirmed online appointment
- The passport application form, filled out
- Your PSA birth certificate on security paper, original and one photocopy
- One photocopy of a valid ID from the DFA's list of accepted IDs (bring the
  original too)
- For married women using their husband's last name: PSA marriage certificate
  on security paper (or Report of Marriage), original and one photocopy

Women keeping their maiden name don't need the marriage certificate.

## Steps
1. Book an appointment on the DFA's online passport appointment site.
2. Prepare the documents above. Check that the name and birth date on your
   PSA birth certificate match your ID.
3. Go to the DFA office you booked on your appointment date. You have to
   appear in person.
4. Submit your form and documents, pay the fee, and have your photo,
   fingerprints and signature taken.
5. Wait for your passport, then claim it (or have it delivered if you chose
   that option).

## Fees
As of 2026-10-26, per the DFA fees page:
- Regular: ₱950
- Expedited: ₱1,200

## Where to apply
At the DFA office you choose when booking: DFA Aseana in Parañaque or a DFA
consular office in your region.

## How long it takes
- Regular: 12 business days
- Expedited: 6 business days in Metro Manila, 7 business days outside
  Metro Manila

## Common problems
- **The PSA birth certificate is blurry or hard to read:** the DFA may ask for
  a copy from your Local Civil Registrar as well.
- **Your details don't match across documents** (wrong spelling, different
  birth date): you'll need extra documents. Check the "discrepancy" section on
  the DFA first-time applicants page before your appointment.
- **Your birth was registered late:** extra documents are required. See the
  DFA first-time applicants page for the list.

## Not covered here
- Renewing a passport: see `passport-renewal-adult`.
- Passports for children under 18: see `passport-minors`.
- Lost or damaged passports: see `passport-lost`.
- Dual citizens and naturalized citizens: requirements differ, check the DFA
  first-time applicants page or contact the DFA directly.
