# Getting a passport for the first time (adults)

## Summary
Book an appointment at passport.gov.ph, then go in person with your PSA
birth certificate, one valid ID and the filled-out application form. Regular
processing costs ₱950 and takes 12 business days. If you need it sooner,
expedited processing costs ₱1,200 and takes 6 to 7 business days.

## Who this is for
Filipinos aged 18 and over applying for their very first passport. If you've
had a passport before, see `passport-renewal-adult`.

## What you need
- A confirmed online appointment from passport.gov.ph
- The application form, filled out
- Your PSA birth certificate on security paper, original and one photocopy
- One valid ID from the DFA's list of accepted IDs, with one photocopy
- For married women using their husband's last name: PSA marriage certificate
  on security paper (or Report of Marriage), original and one photocopy

Women keeping their maiden name don't need the marriage certificate.

## Steps
1. Book an appointment at https://www.passport.gov.ph/. If you qualify for
   the Courtesy Lane, you don't need an appointment.
2. Prepare the documents above. Check that the name and birth date on your ID
   match your PSA birth certificate.
3. Go to the DFA office you booked, on your appointment date. You have to
   appear in person.
4. Submit your form and documents and pay the fee.
5. Claim your passport when it's ready (see `passport-claiming`).

## Fees
As of 2026-10-26, per the DFA fees page:
- Regular: ₱950
- Expedited: ₱1,200

## Where to apply
At the DFA office you pick when booking. The main office is DFA Aseana in
Parañaque.

## How long it takes
- Regular: 12 business days
- Expedited: 6 business days in Metro Manila, 7 business days outside
  Metro Manila

## Common problems
- **Your PSA birth certificate is blurry or unreadable:** bring the Local
  Civil Registrar copy as well.
- **Your ID and PSA birth certificate don't match** (spelling, birth date):
  the DFA asks you to correct the document first so the details match your
  PSA record, for example with a PSA-annotated birth certificate or a
  corrected ID.
- **Your birth was registered late:** bring one more primary ID. If you don't
  have one, bring any two of these: NBI clearance, school records (Form 137,
  transcript or diploma), service record for government employees, or your
  PhilHealth Member Data Record.
- **You have no PSA birth certificate at all:** file a late registration with
  your Local Civil Registrar first.

## Not covered here
- Renewing a passport: see `passport-renewal-adult`.
- Passports for children under 18: see `passport-minors`.
- Naturalized citizens, dual citizens and citizenship by election: extra
  documents apply. Check the DFA adult new applications page, or call the DFA
  at (02) 8651-9400.
