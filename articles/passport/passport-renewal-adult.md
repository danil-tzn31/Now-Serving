# Renewing a passport (adults)

## Summary
Book an online appointment, then go in person with your current ePassport,
a photocopy of its data page and the filled-out application form. Regular
processing costs ₱950 and takes 12 business days.

## Who this is for
Filipinos aged 18 and over who have an ePassport, whether it's still valid or
already expired. If you've never had a passport, see `passport-new-adult`.

## What you need
- A confirmed online appointment
- The passport application form, filled out
- Your current ePassport and a photocopy of its data page
- If your name has changed: the original PSA document that shows it, such as
  your PSA marriage certificate, an annotated birth or marriage certificate,
  or your spouse's death certificate

Married women who keep their maiden name don't need a marriage certificate.

## Steps
1. Book an appointment on the DFA's online passport appointment site.
2. Prepare your current passport, the data page photocopy and any name-change
   documents.
3. Go to the DFA office you booked on your appointment date. You have to
   appear in person.
4. Submit your form and documents and pay the fee.
5. Wait for your new passport, then claim it.

## Fees
As of 2026-10-26, per the DFA fees page:
- Regular: ₱950
- Expedited: ₱1,200

## Where to apply
At the DFA office you choose when booking.

## How long it takes
- Regular: 12 business days
- Expedited: 6 business days in Metro Manila, 7 business days outside
  Metro Manila

## Common problems
- **You lost your passport:** extra documents and a ₱350 penalty apply. See
  `passport-lost`.
- **Your passport is damaged:** bring a notarized affidavit explaining what
  happened. A ₱350 penalty applies.
- **You never claimed a passport issued to you (2010 onwards):** you'll be
  treated as a new applicant and need a Certificate of Unclaimed Passport and
  a notarized affidavit of explanation.
- **Your renewal gets treated as a new application:** this happens in some
  cases. Bring your PSA birth certificate to be safe if your case is unusual.

## Not covered here
- First-time applicants: see `passport-new-adult`.
- Children under 18: see `passport-minors`.
- Dual citizens, naturalized citizens and women reverting to their maiden
  name: extra documents apply. Check the DFA adult renewal page.
