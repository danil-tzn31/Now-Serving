# Renewing a passport (adults)

## Summary
If your current passport is an ePassport, book an appointment and bring it
with a photocopy of its data page and the filled-out form. Older passports
(brown, green or maroon) are processed like a new application and need your
PSA birth certificate and a valid ID.

## Who this is for
Filipinos aged 18 and over who already have a passport, valid or expired. If
you've never had one, see `passport-new-adult`.

## What you need
**If you have an ePassport:**
- A confirmed online appointment from passport.gov.ph
- The application form, filled out
- Your current ePassport and a photocopy of its data page
- If your name has changed: the original PSA document that supports it, such
  as your marriage certificate, an annotated birth or marriage certificate,
  or your spouse's death certificate

**If you have an older non-ePassport** (brown, green or maroon):
- A confirmed online appointment and the filled-out form
- Your old passport and a photocopy of its data page, if you still have it
- Your PSA birth certificate on security paper, original and one photocopy
- One valid ID with one photocopy
- Married women using their husband's last name: PSA marriage certificate,
  original and one photocopy

Women who keep their maiden name don't need a marriage certificate.

## Steps
1. Book an appointment at https://www.passport.gov.ph/. If you qualify for
   the Courtesy Lane, you don't need an appointment.
2. Prepare the documents for your passport type.
3. Go to the DFA office you booked, on your appointment date. You have to
   appear in person.
4. Submit your form and documents and pay the fee.
5. Claim your new passport and bring your old one (see `passport-claiming`).

## Fees
As of 2026-10-26, per the DFA fees page:
- Regular: ₱950
- Expedited: ₱1,200

## Where to apply
At the DFA office you pick when booking.

## How long it takes
- Regular: 12 business days
- Expedited: 6 business days in Metro Manila, 7 business days outside
  Metro Manila

## Common problems
- **You lost your passport:** see `passport-lost`.
- **You never claimed a passport issued to you (2010 onwards):** you're
  treated as a new applicant. Get a Certificate of Unclaimed Passport and a
  notarized affidavit of explanation.
- **You came back to the Philippines on a travel document instead of a
  passport:** bring the original travel document and a notarized affidavit of
  explanation.

## Not covered here
- First-time applicants: see `passport-new-adult`.
- Children under 18: see `passport-minors`.
- Dual citizens and women going back to their maiden name: extra documents
  apply. Check the DFA adult renewal page or call (02) 8651-9400.
