# Claiming your passport

## Summary
Bring your original DFA receipt and an original valid ID, plus your old
passport if you renewed. Family members can claim it for you with an
authorization letter. Passports left unclaimed for one year are cancelled.

## Who this is for
Anyone picking up a finished passport, for themselves or for someone else.

## What you need
**Claiming your own passport:**
- Your original DFA receipt
- An original valid ID
- Your old passport, if this was a renewal

**A family member claiming for you** (parent, brother, sister, spouse or
child, of legal age):
- An authorization letter that says how they're related to you
- Your original DFA receipt
- Valid IDs of both you and the family member, originals and photocopies

**Anyone else claiming for you:**
- An original notarized Special Power of Attorney (SPA) from you
- Valid IDs of both you and the person named in the SPA, originals and
  photocopies

**For a child's passport:**
- The mother: original DFA receipt and her valid ID
- The father: original DFA receipt, his valid ID and PSA documents showing
  he has parental authority over the child

## Steps
1. Wait until your passport is ready at the office where you applied.
2. Bring the documents above to that office.
3. Present them at the releasing area and receive the passport.

## Fees
None stated on the DFA releasing page.

## Where to apply
At the DFA office where you applied. If you chose courier delivery, contact
the courier company directly.

## How long it takes
Not stated on the DFA releasing page. Your passport is ready after the
processing time for your application (12 business days regular, 6 to 7
expedited).

## Common problems
- **You lost your receipt:** bring an original notarized Affidavit of Lost
  Receipt signed by you.
- **You can't pick it up for a long time:** passports unclaimed after one year
  are cancelled, and you'd have to apply again as a new applicant.

## Not covered here
- Delivery status of a passport sent by courier: ask the courier company.
