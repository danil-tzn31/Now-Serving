# Passports for children under 18

## Summary
The child must appear in person with a parent or an authorized adult. Bring
the child's PSA birth certificate, a valid ID for both the child and the
adult, and the filled-out form. Renewals also need the child's current
ePassport.

## Who this is for
Children under 18 getting their first passport or renewing one, and the
parent or adult going with them.

## What you need
**For a first passport:**
- A confirmed online appointment from passport.gov.ph
- The application form, filled out
- The child's PSA birth certificate
- At least one valid ID for the child and one for the accompanying adult

**For a renewal:**
- Everything above
- The child's current ePassport and a photocopy of its data page

**Who can go with the child depends on the birth certificate:**
- **Parents married to each other:** either parent can go. Bring that
  parent's passport or other proof of identity, plus a copy of the other
  parent's passport or ID.
- **Parents not married to each other:** only the mother can go.
- **Someone other than a parent:** they need a notarized Special Power of
  Attorney (SPA) from a parent or legal guardian, with a copy of the parent's
  ID or passport attached, plus their own passport or government ID.

## Steps
1. Book an appointment at https://www.passport.gov.ph/. If you qualify for
   the Courtesy Lane, you don't need an appointment.
2. Prepare the child's documents and the accompanying adult's documents.
3. Go to the DFA office you booked, with the child, on your appointment date.
4. Submit the form and documents and pay the fee.
5. Claim the passport when it's ready (see `passport-claiming`).

## Fees
As of 2026-10-26, per the DFA fees page:
- Regular: ₱950
- Expedited: ₱1,200

## Where to apply
At the DFA office you pick when booking.

## How long it takes
- Regular: 12 business days
- Expedited: 6 business days in Metro Manila, 7 business days outside
  Metro Manila

## Common problems
- **The baby is under 1 and has no PSA birth certificate yet:** if born in the
  Philippines, bring a certified true copy of the Local Civil Registrar birth
  certificate authenticated by the PSA.
- **The birth was registered more than a year late:** add school records or a
  baby book or health record.
- **The child's passport was lost:** a parent, guardian or authorized adult
  must file the police report and sign the notarized affidavit of loss, both
  in English. A ₱350 penalty applies.
- **The child's passport was damaged:** bring a notarized affidavit of
  explanation and the PSA birth certificate. A ₱350 penalty applies and it's
  treated as a new application.

## Not covered here
- Children whose mother has passed away, or whose parents are both absent:
  other relatives or a court-appointed guardian may accompany them, with extra
  documents. Check the DFA minor new applications page or call
  (02) 8651-9400.
- SPAs signed abroad: see the DFA page for notarization and apostille rules.
